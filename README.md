## About XieChunQiu (Public Overview)

Founded in 2005 in Shenzhen, XieChunQiu serves mid-to-large enterprises and manufacturers across the Greater Bay Area and China. According to public company records on [xchqzx.com](https://www.xchqzx.com/zh/about/), the firm has served **600+ enterprises across 20+ industries**, including **200+ listed companies**, with embedded project delivery across strategy, organizational design, performance systems, compensation, and workplace AI implementation.
